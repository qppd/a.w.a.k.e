LM2596 Buck Converter Enclosure by Estep on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4096861

Summary:
With this LM2596 Buck Converter Enclosure, you can measure and adjust the voltage, without opening it. There are two versions. Version 02 is for already soldered cables and when the cabels are meant to go to the back instead of the sides.2 screws are needed to Mount this enclosure, but if 4 screws are being used, you can take off the top of the enclosure separately.
